GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do braiding services cost?

Prices depend on the hairstyle, length, size and hair requirements. Please check Booksy for listed prices or contact us for a quote.

Can I book an appointment for my child?

Yes. Children's braiding is available. Please check the available children's styles and appointment requirements before booking.

How should I prepare my hair?

Please contact us before your appointment to confirm whether your hair should be washed, detangled or prepared in a specific way.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Please review the cancellation and rescheduling terms on Booksy and follow the instructions associated with your appointment.

Can I request a custom hairstyle?

Yes. Contact us with your preferred style and reference photos so we can discuss availability, timing and pricing.
